REAL-FLIGHT VS SIMULATION COMPARISON REPORT
===========================================

Datasets
--------
Real: /home/randilsk/FYP/multi_uav_sim/results/real_2026-08-31_18-03-14
Simulation: /home/randilsk/FYP/multi_uav_sim/results/sim_run_2026-09-02_02-22-31

Availability
------------
Directly comparable signals: arrival_timestamp, latitude, longitude, relative_altitude, ground_speed, heading, telemetry_interarrival.
Comparable after conversion/alignment: elapsed_time, roll, pitch, yaw, waypoint_progress.
Not directly comparable or insufficient: mavlink_sequence, snr_db, signal_dbm, radio_status, packet_loss, rtt, throughput.

Time alignment
--------------
Mission time zero uses first two consecutive GPS samples at or above 0.5 m relative altitude. Real alignment occurred at source elapsed 238.977 s and simulation alignment at 41.607 s. The shared comparison interval is 0 to 88.802 s and no data are extrapolated.

Coordinate alignment
--------------------
Both WGS84 GPS tracks were recomputed in a common ENU frame about (6.077372074, 80.190757751, 0.000 m). Dataset-specific local-coordinate columns were not compared.

Results
-------
The trajectories have a horizontal RMSE of 4.371 m and P95 separation of 8.206 m over the shared interval. Recorded horizontal distances are 31.184 m real and 27.969 m simulated.

The time-aligned horizontal error above includes both spatial path mismatch and mission timing differences. Independently of timestamps, bidirectional point-to-polyline geometry gives a symmetric mean path error of 0.674 m, symmetric RMSE of 0.736 m, and Hausdorff-style maximum deviation of 1.401 m. These geometry-only values measure path-shape similarity and do not pair samples by time.

Geometry against the commanded mission path is also evaluated without temporal pairing. Real-flight mean/RMSE/P95 path errors are 0.214/0.457/1.280 m; simulation mean/RMSE/P95 path errors are 0.744/0.777/1.093 m. These are point-to-commanded-polyline cross-track distances and do not replace the time-aligned real-versus-simulation RMSE.

Relative-altitude RMSE is 0.726 m. Measured-groundspeed RMSE is 0.238 m/s. Circular GPS-heading mean absolute error is 6.165 degrees.

The common final target was reached at mission time 80.386 s in the real flight and 89.146 s in simulation. The real mission used multiple AUTO waypoints while simulation used one GUIDED target, so intermediate waypoint timing is not treated as equivalent.

Application telemetry continuity
--------------------------------
Within the shared mission interval, mean equivalent GLOBAL_POSITION_INT arrival rates are 2.00099159 Hz real and 1.93294494 Hz simulated. Gaps are timing/continuity observations, not packet loss. Simulation MAVLink sequence numbers were unavailable, so sequence-loss estimates are not compared.

Network limitations
-------------------
Insufficient real radio samples for RF time-series validation.
Simulation SNR and signal level are characterized separately. No real SNR was invented. Packet loss, RTT, and throughput are unavailable as defensible comparable measurements.

Interpretation
--------------
These results quantify agreement and disagreement for the recorded runs; they do not by themselves establish general simulation validation. Differences include vehicle behavior, sampling, control mode, and mission structure as well as model fidelity.
