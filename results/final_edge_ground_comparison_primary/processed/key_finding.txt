Across three independent runs per mode, Edge processing completed 100% of locally published official frames, compared with 76.7% mean complete-frame processing for Ground. The mean of run-level median latency was 182.4 ms for Edge and 507.9 ms for Ground, while YOLO inference time remained similar. Ground JPEG compression and decoding averaged only 4.6 ms combined. In the separately labelled D4 evaluation, JPEG quality 5 reduced recall from 0.811 to 0.172 and F1 from 0.843 to 0.293. Edge required higher simulation-host CPU, indicating a resource trade-off. Under the tested conditions, Edge was preferable for latency-sensitive perception, while Ground remains potentially suitable when communication and image quality are sufficient or aerial compute is constrained.
